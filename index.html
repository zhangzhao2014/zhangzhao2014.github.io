面向半导体、汽车、机器人等国家战略和高新领域，发展新理论、新方法、新工具，探究“材料和结构在不同服役场景下的物理行为和演化机制”，促进相关领域科学和技术进步。

Bridging fundamental mechanics and engineering applications in high-tech sectors including semiconductors, new energy vehicles, and robotics. My work focuses on solving complex physical problems related to "constitutive behaviors, structural evolution, and system reliability" under extreme conditions. By integrating multi-scale simulation, advanced testing, and AI, I deliver end-to-end solutions from underlying mechanism discovery to industrial multi-objective optimization.

研究方向 01 /半导体封装Core Direction 01 / Advanced Packaging Mechanics

研究集成电路封装结构中互连材料在不同工况下的本构行为、多场失效机制及可靠性评估方法。研究封装材料与结构的数字孪生模型构建理论和方法，并结合人工智能算法，实现物理驱动的封装材料和结构快速可靠性建模。 Tackling complex constitutive behaviors and multi-physics failure mechanisms of interconnect materials in IC advanced packaging. Leveraging AI/ML algorithms to build digital twins, enabling physics-driven rapid reliability design and precise life prediction.

研究方向 02 / 轻量化抗冲击设计Core Direction 02 / Extreme Impact & Metamaterials

揭示新材料和新结构在动态冲击下的物理行为和变形机制。结合多目标优化方法，促进相关产品在轻量化、抗冲击、模态、散热等多性能的权衡设计。 Unveiling the deformation and energy-absorption mechanisms of novel materials and topological structures (e.g., metamaterials) under dynamic impact. Utilizing isogeometric analysis and multi-objective optimization to achieve ultimate trade-offs among lightweighting, crashworthiness, and fatigue resistance.

研究方向 03 / 机器人动力学Core Direction 03 / Robot Contact Dynamics

研究机器人在多类型场景下交互的动力学建模理论，提供机器人与外界交互的底层作用力建模方法，促进机器人更鲁棒和更安全的设计、交互与应用。 Breaking through dynamic modeling bottlenecks in physical interactions between robots and complex environments. Focusing on rough-interface contact theory, rigid-flexible multibody dynamics, and interaction algorithms to provide fundamental mechanical support for robust and safe robotic operations.

场景 01 / 半导体封装Scenario 01 / Semiconductor Packaging

芯片封装可靠性Packaging Reliability & Process Yield

解决的问题与交付Problems Solved & Deliverables
  • 本构建模与参数提取：针对新型封装互连材料，提供更接近真实状态的本构模型构建和参数辨识方案，解决仿真失真的痛点。 Constitutive Modeling: Providing high-precision testing and parameter extraction for novel solders/adhesives, eliminating the "garbage in, garbage out" simulation dilemma.
  • 机理揭示、失效预警与寿命预测：基于多尺度及多物理场（热-电-力等）服役状态下的数字孪生模型，揭示相关设计/工艺机理和准则，缩短试错周期。 Failure Warning & Life Prediction: Electro-thermo-mechanical simulations to pre-identify cracking and delamination risks in BGA/WLCSP, significantly reducing trial-and-error cycles.
场景 02 / 防护安全Scenario 02 / Automotive Industry

防护安全与轻量化降本Crash Safety & Lightweighting

解决的问题与交付Problems Solved & Deliverables
  • 整车合规数字化重建：针对国标/C-NCAP/E-NCAP等标准，提供高保真部件/整车碰撞仿真分析，发展仿真理论和方法，保障高星级评级。 Digital Crash Reconstruction: High-fidelity vehicle and battery pack crash simulations aligned with C-NCAP/E-NCAP standards to secure high safety ratings.
  • 性能与成本权衡：运用先进仿真和优化方法，促进产品多性能优化设计（安全、强度、模态、轻量化、美观），提高研发效率，实现产品结构减重降本。 Performance-Cost Balance: Integrated topology-shape optimization to reduce structural weight and cost while satisfying stiffness, NVH, and Pedestrian Protection (HIC) targets.

  • 仿生学轻量化设计：针对机械臂与关节支架，提供面向3D打印的拓扑减重和散热设计，降低电机负荷，提升动态响应。 Generative Lightweight Design: Topology optimization for manipulators and joints geared towards 3D printing, reducing motor payload and enhancing dynamic response.
